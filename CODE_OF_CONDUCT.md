# Code of conduct

JonaAI follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- Be welcoming, kind and patient. People come to Earth Observation from many fields and levels of experience.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Assume good faith.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks, in public or in private.
- Don't publish anyone's private information without their permission.

It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability"), and say in the title that it's a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn or ban contributors, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
